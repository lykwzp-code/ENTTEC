# ENTTEC Product Specialist Strategy Lab

A working product-strategy portfolio exploring how ENTTEC could connect creator-facing experimentation, professional-scale lighting tools, artist co-creation, AI-assisted creative workflows, and community-led product discovery.

> **Status:** independent concept work for interview / product thinking.  
> This repository is **not** an official ENTTEC roadmap and does not claim that proposed features or programs are planned internally.

## North-star idea

**Help creators turn artistic intent into light — then scale the ideas that prove useful into professional production tools.**

The central product thesis is:

- **EMU** can become ENTTEC's creator-facing experimentation platform: fast, musician-first, expressive, and safe for trying new interaction ideas.
- **ELM** can remain the professional spatial-production platform: large-scale, precise, reliable, and suitable for touring, installation, architectural and integrator workflows.
- Mature creative capabilities discovered with artists can **graduate** from EMU experiments into production-grade capabilities in ELM when they make sense for professional users.
- A longer-term **shared creative engine** could reduce duplicated product work while allowing EMU and ELM to expose the same underlying capabilities through very different UX.

## Current public product facts used as grounding

The proposals in this repo are grounded in ENTTEC's public product information as of October 2026:

- [EMU](https://www.enttec.com/product/dmx-lighting-control-software/emu-sound-to-light-controller/) is positioned for musicians, DJs and producers, with audio-reactive control, MIDI, DAW integration and up to 32 universes.
- [ELM 2026](https://www.enttec.com/product/dmx-lighting-control-software/pixel-mapping-software/) is positioned for pixel mapping at professional and architectural scale, with 2D/3D mapping, very large output scale, live media/effects, BPM/audio capabilities, Device Manager and Sentry.
- ENTTEC states that **130+ GPU shader effects in ELM 2026 were ported from EMU**, which provides an observable precedent for creative capability transfer across the software portfolio.
- [S-PLAY](https://support.enttec.com/show-controllers/s-play-70092/an-overview-of-s-play-automation) covers standalone show-control / automation use cases for permanent installations.

Everything beyond those published facts should be treated as a **product hypothesis to validate**, not a statement about ENTTEC's internal strategy.
