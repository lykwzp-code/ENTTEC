# ENTTEC Product Specialist Strategy

An independent product-strategy portfolio built around one question:

> **How can ENTTEC help creators turn artistic intent into light — and turn the strongest creator insights into scalable professional product value?**

> **Status:** interview / product-thinking work.  
> This repository is **not** an official ENTTEC roadmap. Company facts, product hypotheses and interview unknowns are kept separate.

---

## The complete story

This repository is designed to tell one continuous story, not present a collection of disconnected ideas.

### 1. Start from the creator problem
Light should not merely react to music. It can participate in artistic expression.

A creator may know exactly what they want the audience to feel, but still struggle to translate that intent into lighting logic and control parameters.

**Core problem:**

**creative intent → ? → visual language → executable lighting**

[Read: 01 — Product Thesis](docs/01_PRODUCT_THESIS.md)

### 2. Give EMU and ELM complementary roles
A working portfolio hypothesis:

> **EMU discovers new creative language.  
> ELM scales selected proven creative language into professional production.**

EMU stays creator-first and experimental. ELM stays professional, spatial, deterministic and scalable. S-PLAY / hardware can support deployment and unattended operation.

[Read: 02 — Portfolio Strategy](docs/02_PORTFOLIO_STRATEGY.md)

### 3. Validate the market before building the big vision
Do not begin with a large AI project or celebrity partnership.

Start with a low-cost creator-market test:

> **Reimagined in Light** — reinterpret rights-cleared music through light, publish the creative process, and observe whether audiences and creators want to make this kind of work themselves.

Older catalogue music, K-pop visual culture and dance creators can be useful entry points because they combine fandom, choreography, video and visual identity.

[Read: 03 — Creator Market Entry](docs/03_CREATOR_MARKET_ENTRY.md)

### 4. Turn successful content into co-creation
If the content format produces real creator demand, evolve it into **EMU Artist Lab**.

Principle:

> **Design partner first, endorsement second.**

Artists bring real songs and real intent. Product friction becomes research. Community comments become hypotheses. If a requested capability does not exist yet, the limitation can become a future return episode after the product improves.

[Read: 04 — Artist Lab & Community](docs/04_ARTIST_LAB_COMMUNITY.md)

### 5. Add AI only where a validated problem needs it
AI is not the starting point.

If creators repeatedly struggle to translate artistic language into lighting logic, AI can be tested as a:

> **creative translator, not an automatic taste-maker**

The product should not decide what the music means. It should help the artist express what they mean.

[Read: 05 — AI Creative Translator](docs/05_AI_CREATIVE_TRANSLATOR.md)

### 6. Define what the Product Specialist actually owns
The role should connect:

**user / creator  
→ problem definition  
→ hypothesis  
→ experiment  
→ product decision  
→ launch / adoption  
→ learning**

The strategic value is not “support more products.” It is moving from **customer input to product decision participation**.

[Read: 06 — Product Specialist Operating Model](docs/06_PRODUCT_SPECIALIST_OPERATING_MODEL.md)

### 7. Execute with evidence, not enthusiasm
The first six months follow:

> **Learn → Discover → Experiment → Prove**

The goal is not to defend the original idea. A good result may include killing weak hypotheses.

[Read: 07 — 180-Day Execution Plan](docs/07_180_DAY_EXECUTION_PLAN.md)

### 8. Keep facts, hypotheses and unknowns separate
The repository deliberately distinguishes:

- published ENTTEC product facts
- proposed strategy
- questions that must be validated in interview / internally

[Read: 08 — Assumptions & Validation](docs/08_ASSUMPTIONS_VALIDATION.md)

## The 30-second thesis

> I’m interested in the gap between artistic intent and lighting control. My hypothesis is that EMU could be a creator-facing place to test new ways of translating music into visual performance, while selected mature ideas could later scale into professional workflows in ELM. I would validate that cheaply first — through creator content and adjacent markets such as dance — then move into deeper artist co-creation, and only use AI where real user evidence shows it helps.

Not:

> “I already know ENTTEC’s roadmap.”

## 完整故事线

这个仓库不是一堆彼此独立的 idea，而是一条连续的产品故事。

### 1. 从创作者问题开始

光不应该只是跟随音乐变化，它也可以参与艺术表达。

创作者可能很清楚自己希望观众感受到什么，但仍然不知道如何把这种意图翻译成灯光逻辑和控制参数。

**核心问题：**

**艺术意图 → ？ → 视觉语言 → 可执行的灯光**

[阅读：01 — Product Thesis](docs/01_PRODUCT_THESIS.md)

### 2. 给 EMU 和 ELM 建立互补定位

一个值得验证的 portfolio hypothesis：

> **EMU 负责发现新的创作语言。  
> ELM 负责把其中经过验证的能力扩展成专业级生产工具。**

EMU 保持 creator-first 与实验性；ELM 保持专业、空间化、可重复、可规模化。S-PLAY 与硬件生态则可以承接部署和无人值守运行。

[阅读：02 — Portfolio Strategy](docs/02_PORTFOLIO_STRATEGY.md)

### 3. 在做“大愿景”之前先验证市场

不要一开始就做大型 AI 项目，也不要先请大牌艺人。

先做一个低成本 creator-market test：

> **Reimagined in Light** —— 用已经完成授权的音乐做新的光影视觉诠释，公开创作过程，再观察观众和创作者是否真的愿意自己尝试。

旧歌、K-pop 视觉文化、舞蹈创作者都是很好的早期切口，因为它们同时连接 fandom、舞蹈、视频和视觉身份。

[阅读：03 — Creator Market Entry](docs/03_CREATOR_MARKET_ENTRY.md)

### 4. 把成功的内容形式升级成共创

如果这种内容真的带来 creator demand，就进一步做 **EMU Artist Lab**。

原则是：

> **先把音乐人当 design partner，再把他们当 endorsement。**

音乐人带来真实歌曲和真实意图。产品摩擦变成用户研究；评论区反馈变成 hypothesis；如果当下实现不了某个功能，这个 limitation 甚至可以成为未来“回归集”的剧情。

[阅读：04 — Artist Lab & Community](docs/04_ARTIST_LAB_COMMUNITY.md)

### 5. 只有在问题被验证以后才引入 AI

AI 不是故事的起点。

如果创作者反复遇到“艺术表达难以翻译成灯光语言”的问题，才去测试 AI 是否适合作为：

> **creative translator，而不是 automatic taste-maker**

产品不应该替音乐人决定音乐意味着什么，而应该帮助音乐人表达他们自己想表达的东西。

[阅读：05 — AI Creative Translator](docs/05_AI_CREATIVE_TRANSLATOR.md)

### 6. 明确 Product Specialist 到底应该负责什么

这个角色应该连接：

**用户 / 创作者  
→ 问题定义  
→ hypothesis  
→ experiment  
→ product decision  
→ launch / adoption  
→ learning**

它的战略价值不是“支持更多产品”，而是从 **customer input 进入 product decision participation**。

[阅读：06 — Product Specialist Operating Model](docs/06_PRODUCT_SPECIALIST_OPERATING_MODEL.md)

### 7. 用证据执行，而不是靠兴奋感

前六个月遵循：

> **Learn → Discover → Experiment → Prove**

目标不是证明最初的 idea 一定正确。一个好的结果也可以包括：尽早杀掉错误 hypothesis。

[阅读：07 — 180-Day Execution Plan](docs/07_180_DAY_EXECUTION_PLAN.md)

### 8. 把事实、假设和未知项分开

仓库会明确区分：

- ENTTEC 已公开确认的产品事实
- 本仓库提出的产品假设
- 必须在面试或内部进一步确认的问题

[阅读：08 — Assumptions & Validation](docs/08_ASSUMPTIONS_VALIDATION.md)

## 30 秒核心 thesis

> 我感兴趣的是“艺术意图”和“灯光控制”之间的 translation gap。我的 hypothesis 是：EMU 可以成为一个 creator-facing 的实验入口，用来测试新的音乐视觉表达方式；其中真正成熟、有专业价值的能力，再逐步进入 ELM。  
> 我不会一开始就做大型 AI 功能或请大牌艺人，而会先通过内容和 dance creator 这类邻近市场做低成本验证，再升级到 artist co-creation；只有当真实用户证据说明 AI 确实有价值时，才把它作为 creative translator 引入。
