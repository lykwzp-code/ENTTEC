# 08 — Assumptions & Interview Validation

## Purpose

Keep three things separate:

1. **published ENTTEC product facts**
2. **product hypotheses in this repository**
3. **unknowns that must be validated in interview or internally**

This prevents a product idea from turning into an unsupported claim about ENTTEC’s strategy.

---

## 1. Public facts used as grounding

Based on ENTTEC’s public product materials used in this repository:

### EMU
- creator / musician-oriented lighting-control software
- audio-reactive workflows
- MIDI / DAW integration
- smaller-scale creator use than ELM

### ELM 2026
- professional pixel-mapping / spatial-production platform
- 2D / 3D mapping
- large-scale output
- live media / effects
- professional / installation capabilities
- includes many GPU shader effects ported from EMU

### S-PLAY
- standalone show-control / scheduling / triggering for permanent or unattended use cases

These facts justify asking questions about portfolio relationships. They do **not** prove the strategy proposed here.

---

## 2. Core hypotheses to validate

- EMU could deliberately act as a creator-facing experimentation platform.
- creator-facing content could open new markets, including dance / performance creators.
- Artist Lab could be useful product-discovery infrastructure.
- repeated creator needs could graduate selectively into ELM.
- a shared creative layer may eventually make sense.
- AI could help translate creative intent into editable visual logic.
- Product Specialist could own part of the discovery → experiment → decision loop.

None of these should be presented as existing ENTTEC commitments.

---

## 3. Role questions

### Why did the role broaden?
> Since the role is now broader than EMU, what product problems or opportunities made the team decide to broaden it?

### Ownership
> What would I personally own in this role rather than support?

### Product decision participation
> Could you walk me through a recent product decision a Product Specialist influenced, and who made the final call?

### Scope
> Does “not limited to EMU” mainly mean supporting more products, or taking broader product responsibility across software and hardware applications?

### User access
> Would this role have direct access to users such as musicians, designers or integrators for product discovery?

### Experimentation
> If we wanted to test a small workflow or prototype with users before committing major Engineering resources, could this role lead that experiment?

### Resources
> Which Engineering, Product, Design or Marketing resources would support this role, and how are priorities decided when those teams are busy?

### Growth
> What would you expect this person to own independently after the first year?


## 3A. What these role questions are actually testing

These questions are not generic interview questions. Together they test whether the job provides four things: **Access, Ownership, Resources, and Decision & Accountability.**

### Direct user access
Ask this to learn whether you can hear and observe real users directly, rather than receiving only filtered feedback through Sales or Support.

**Strong answer:** regular interviews, user sessions, customer visits, beta groups.  
**Weak answer:** “Sales will tell you what customers need.”

### Independent problem space
Ask this to learn whether you will own a persistent product problem, not only execute miscellaneous tasks.

**Strong answer:** “You would own creator onboarding / this workflow / this product domain.”  
**Weak answer:** “You’ll help wherever needed.”

### Experiment authority
Ask this to learn whether you can turn a hypothesis into a test.

**Strong answer:** the role can run small pilots, prototypes or user tests.  
**Weak answer:** all ideas must wait for a formal roadmap slot.

### Engineering capacity
Ask this to learn whether validated product ideas can receive actual implementation support.

**Strong answer:** named Engineering partner, sprint / prototype capacity, clear priority process.  
**Weak answer:** “Engineering is usually very busy, but you can submit requests.”

### Roadmap participation
Ask this to learn whether the role influences what gets built, rather than only supporting things after decisions are made.

**Strong answer:** the role brings evidence into prioritisation discussions.  
**Weak answer:** roadmap decisions happen elsewhere and Product Specialist handles enablement afterwards.

### Broader scope: ownership vs support
Ask this because “not limited to EMU” can mean two very different things.

**Good meaning:** broader product responsibility and decision participation.  
**Bad meaning:** more products to support reactively.

### 12-month domain ownership
Ask this to learn whether the role compounds into a recognised area of responsibility.

**Strong answer:** there is a credible path toward a named domain / feature family / user journey.  
**Weak answer:** growth means becoming the most knowledgeable general support person.

### Discovery-to-outcome continuity
Ask this to learn whether you can see whether your product judgement was actually right.

**Strong answer:** stay involved through launch, adoption and iteration.  
**Weak answer:** collect needs, hand them off, then move to the next request.

A practical summary:

> **Access tells me whether I can learn.  
> Ownership tells me whether I can focus.  
> Resources tell me whether I can act.  
> Decision & accountability tell me whether I can build product judgement.**


## 3A. 这些岗位问题到底在测什么

这些不是泛泛的“面试反问”。

它们合起来是在验证四件事：

**Access、Ownership、Resources、Decision & Accountability。**

### Direct user access
想确认你能不能直接听到、看到真实用户，而不是永远接受 Sales / Support 过滤后的需求。

**强信号：** 有定期 interview、user session、customer visit、beta group。  
**弱信号：** “Sales 会告诉你客户需要什么。”

### Independent problem space
想确认你有没有一块持续负责的产品问题，而不是每天接不同杂事。

**强信号：** “你会负责 creator onboarding / 某个 workflow / 某个 product domain。”  
**弱信号：** “哪里需要你就帮哪里。”

### Experiment authority
想确认你能不能把 hypothesis 变成 test。

**强信号：** 可以做小 pilot、prototype、user test。  
**弱信号：** 所有 idea 都只能等正式 roadmap slot。

### Engineering capacity
想确认已经被验证的 idea 有没有实际实现资源。

**强信号：** 有明确 Engineering partner、prototype / sprint capacity、priority process。  
**弱信号：** “Engineering 平时很忙，不过你可以提需求。”

### Roadmap participation
想确认你能不能 influence what gets built，而不是 roadmap 定完后再做 support。

**强信号：** Product Specialist 可以带 evidence 进入 prioritisation discussion。  
**弱信号：** roadmap 在别处决定，你只负责 launch / training / enablement。

### Broader scope：ownership 还是 support
因为 “not limited to EMU” 可能代表完全不同的两种工作。

**好的含义：** 更大的 product responsibility 与 decision participation。  
**坏的含义：** 更多产品需要 reactive support。

### 12-month domain ownership
想确认岗位是不是会随着时间形成一块真正属于你的责任领域。

**强信号：** 一年后有明确 domain / feature family / user journey 可以 ownership。  
**弱信号：** 成长只是变成最熟所有产品的 support generalist。

### Discovery-to-outcome continuity
想确认你最终能不能知道自己的产品判断到底对不对。

**强信号：** 从 discovery 一直跟到 launch、adoption 和 iteration。  
**弱信号：** 收需求、交出去，然后马上进入下一单。

可以压缩成一句：

> **Access 决定我能不能学到真实问题；  
> Ownership 决定我能不能持续深入；  
> Resources 决定我能不能行动；  
> Decision & accountability 决定我能不能真正积累 product judgement。**

---

## 4. Strategy questions

### EMU / ELM
> I noticed ELM 2026 includes effects that originated in EMU. How does the team currently think about innovation across EMU and ELM?

### Creator market
> Do you currently see musicians as the main creator audience for EMU, or are there adjacent groups — for example performance creators, dance studios or content creators — that the team is interested in?

### Artist collaboration
> Has ENTTEC used artists mainly for case studies / marketing, or have creator partnerships also influenced product decisions?

### AI
> Has the team discussed using AI above the current audio-reactive layer — for example, helping creators translate artistic intent or song structure into an editable lighting starting point?

Do not assume the answer is yes.

---

## 5. Strong signals

- named product / domain ownership
- direct user access
- Product Specialist participates in product decisions
- ability to run small experiments
- real Engineering access
- specific examples of roadmap influence
- responsibility grows after 12–24 months
- clear distinction between support and ownership

---

## 6. Weak / ambiguous signals

These require follow-up:

- “you’ll touch many products”
- “we’re entrepreneurial”
- “everyone wears many hats”
- “you’ll work globally”
- “there are lots of opportunities”

They may be true, but they do not establish Product Authority.

---

## 7. Negative signals

- almost all work is reactive support
- no one can explain decision rights
- no protected discovery / product time
- no direct user access
- no mechanism for Engineering capacity
- scope grows only by adding support workload
- ideas are collected but not validated or decided
- product ownership exists only in title

---

## 8. Interview principle

Do not pitch the entire repository.

Sequence:

1. ask about the actual role
2. listen for the real problem
3. share one relevant observation
4. test one hypothesis
5. expand only if the interviewer engages

The goal is to learn whether this is the kind of environment where the strategy could be tested — not to prove that the strategy is already correct.

## 8. Interview principle

不要把整个仓库一次讲完。

顺序：

1. 先问真实岗位
2. 听清真正问题
3. 分享一个 relevant observation
4. 测一个 hypothesis
5. 对方有兴趣再展开

目标不是证明这套策略已经正确，而是确认：这个环境是否允许你去真正测试它。
