# 08 — Product Hypotheses & Evidence Standards

This file records **unproven project hypotheses** and how they might be validated. It does not describe ENTTEC's approved roadmap or established user demand.

## H1 — First success can be made easier

**Hypothesis:** A musician, dancer or content creator with limited lighting knowledge can create a useful expressive look through an outcome-first template flow.

**Test:** observe beginner and experienced users attempting the same goal with current controls and a prototype flow.

**Evidence:** setup completion, time to first real result, level of help required, repeatability and first customization.

**Counter-evidence:** successful demos require a technician; physical setup friction overwhelms UI gains.

---

## H2 — Templates can teach, not trap

**Hypothesis:** A user can start with a preset, inspect its logic, change one section and continue into deeper control without rebuilding the project.

**Test:** template → creator edit → advanced control usability sessions.

**Evidence:** users successfully explain and modify choices and use the system again.

**Counter-evidence:** users remain preset-only, edits are opaque, or advanced workflows become worse.

---

## H3 — Dance / studio / livestream creators may be incremental users

**Hypothesis:** At least some visual-performance creators are willing to adopt and pay for physical lighting workflows they did not previously think of as lighting products.

**Test:** separate Guangzhou Dance/Creator and Livestream/MCN pilots, including actual hands-on participation.

**Evidence:** partner repeat use, viable setup economics, paid intent or real budget, self-service ability and referrals.

**Counter-evidence:** high social engagement but little product adoption; hardware and support costs make the proposition unattractive.

---

## H4 — Content can lead to qualified discovery

**Hypothesis:** Reimagined in Light content can attract creators through the finished work and lead them to learn, use and share EMU.

**Test:** rights-cleared episodes with trackable calls to action.

**Evidence:** qualified downloads, tutorial/project completion, creator enquiries and repeat creation.

**Counter-evidence:** views come only from song nostalgia, fandom or sponsored dancers.

---

## H5 — Music-to-Light grammar may be reusable

**Hypothesis:** Research with musicians can reveal useful creative primitives that are portable across several artists without imposing one universal mapping.

**Test:** artist-led song decomposition, visual alternatives, creator evaluations, repeated cross-artist research.

**Evidence:** recurring problems/relationships, improvements in alignment and editable output.

**Counter-evidence:** the vocabulary is too artist-specific to reduce creation friction.

---

## H6 — AI may help only after manual research

**Hypothesis:** If translation from artistic intent to lighting logic repeatedly causes friction, an AI-assisted but editable brief or proposal may reduce it.

**Test:** compare a manually facilitated visual brief with AI-assisted alternatives; evaluate creator authorship and quality.

**Evidence:** time saved, better creator comprehension, repeated usage, acceptable output and cost.

**Counter-evidence:** generic or clichéd looks, unreliable fixture output, poor user trust, no gain over simple templates.

---

## H7 — Some creators may progress within the ENTTEC ecosystem

**Hypothesis:** Users who start with EMU might later adopt relevant ENTTEC hardware and, where justified by project scale, ELM.

**Test:** longitudinal creator cohorts and real setup expansion observations.

**Evidence:** hardware attachment, second/expanded setups, professional use-case migrations.

**Counter-evidence:** users never need larger rigs, encounter integration barriers or prefer unrelated professional products.

**Important:** ELM is not automatically the next step for every EMU user; the products serve different work.

---

## Shared safeguards

- **Not all creators want the same kind of expression.**
- **Physical hardware creates an activation barrier that video-editing software does not have.**
- **Existing professional users must not lose precision or reliability.**
- **CapCut/剪映 interoperability is a hypothesis, not a confirmed plugin API integration.**
- **Older music still needs the appropriate rights for commercial video use.**
- **Community enthusiasm should generate questions, not directly determine roadmap priority.**
- **Do not claim an artist or livestream team uses ENTTEC merely because a concept is inspired by their style.**

# 中文版

## 08 — 产品假设与证据标准

这份文档只记录**尚待验证的产品设想**，不是 ENTTEC 已批准 roadmap，也不代表目前已存在足够市场证据。

### H1 — 新手更快完成第一件作品

**假设：** 不熟悉灯控的音乐人、舞者或内容创作者可以通过 outcome-first template 快速完成有效的光影表达。

**验证：** 新手与熟手在现有流程和原型流程下完成相同任务。

**看：** 成功率、首次结果时间、需要多少专家协助、是否愿意再次使用。

**反证：** UI 再简单也离不开专职灯光人员，物理硬件接入仍是最大障碍。

### H2 — Template 可以教育用户，也可以深入修改

**假设：** 用户从模板进入，能看懂、调整段落，再深入专业控制，不必推倒重来。

**验证：** template → Creator edit → Advanced 控制连续测试。

**看：** 是否能解释并修改视觉决策，是否二次使用。

**反证：** 模板成为不可编辑的黑盒，反而损害高级用户工作流。

### H3 — 舞团、练舞房、团播可能是增量市场

**假设：** 一些表演类创作者愿意真正采用并为实体灯光表达付费。

**验证：** 广州 Creator/Dance 和 Livestream/MCN 两组试点。

**看：** 重复使用、商业预算、硬件成本、是否能自行操作、转介绍。

**反证：** 视频很受欢迎，但没人持续使用 EMU；支持/硬件成本超过价值。

### H4 — 内容可以带来有效新用户

**假设：** Reimagined in Light 可以先用作品吸引人，再引导学习和使用 EMU。

**验证：** 使用已授权音乐，设置可追踪的 tutorial / product 路径。

**看：** 有效下载、教程完成、继续创作。

**反证：** 播放量仅来自情怀粉丝，产品转化很弱。

### H5 — Visual Grammar 可能有可复用价值

**假设：** 与音乐人拆歌可以发现跨创作者有价值的表达 primitive，而不是只有一对一固定映射。

**验证：** 不同音乐人独立拆解、做多个视觉版本、评价与比较。

**看：** 重复关系、沟通效率、可编辑结果。

**反证：** 所有关系都过于个人化，无法简化操作。

### H6 — AI 只有在人工研究以后才值得加入

**假设：** 如果意图转译持续困难，可编辑 AI brief / proposal 能改善流程。

**验证：** 人工 brief 与 AI 辅助的对比。

**看：** 时间、可理解性、真实审美匹配、成本与再次使用。

**反证：** 视觉俗套、技术输出不可靠、用户不信任、不如简单模板。

### H7 — 用户可能沿 ENTTEC 产品生态升级

**假设：** 部分 EMU 用户会升级实体硬件，并在项目真正需要时进入 ELM。

**验证：** 持续跟踪真实用户项目与设备变化。

**看：** 硬件升级、重复购买、专业化项目。

**反证：** 用户并不需要大系统、迁移成本高或更倾向其他工具。

**重要：** ELM 并不是每一个 EMU 用户的必经升级路线，它们服务的工作场景本就不同。

### 共同边界

- 创作者没有统一的“正确视觉表达”。
- 实体硬件门槛比视频剪辑高很多。
- 不能为简化新手操作而牺牲现有专业用户能力。
- 与剪映/CapCut 的深度集成不能假定已有公开插件 API。
- 旧歌仍需处理商业内容版权。
- 社区评论提供研究假设，不直接决定 roadmap。
- 概念参考某音乐人或团播团队，不代表对方真实使用 ENTTEC。
