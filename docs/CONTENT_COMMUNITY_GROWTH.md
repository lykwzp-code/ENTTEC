# Content, Community & Creator Growth

## Principle

**Product discovery and marketing can share the same creative process.**

The program should not hide product limitations. Honest iteration can become part of the brand.

---

## 1. Channel roles

### YouTube — full story
20–30 minute episode:
- artist intent
- workflow
- experimentation
- failures
- product discussion
- final audiovisual work

Goal:

> “How did they think and build this?”

### TikTok / Reels / Shorts — spectacle
30–90 second cuts:
- strongest finished light-show / MV sequence
- one surprising transformation
- one creator quote

Goal:

> “I didn’t know lighting could feel like this.”

### Product tutorials — practical depth
10–15 minute:
- recreate one technique from the episode
- project / preset where appropriate
- explain why the creative choice was made

Goal:

> “I want to try this.”

---

## 2. Funnel

**spectacle → curiosity → learning → creation → sharing**

Avoid:

**feature announcement → tutorial → silence**

---

## 3. Comments as discovery

Do not let comments directly vote the roadmap.

Treat comments as hypotheses.

Example comment:

> “Can it understand lyrics?”

Product response:

> “Interesting — is the underlying need about lyrical meaning, song sections, or wanting the lighting to follow a narrative? We’d like to test this with more creators.”

Possible transparent statuses:
- exploring
- testing
- added to exploration backlog
- not planned
- released

Important language:

> “We’re keeping this in our exploration backlog, but it is not a release commitment.”

---

## 4. Community co-creation

Occasionally publish work-in-progress options:

- A / B visual direction
- two different transition models
- sync vs drift
- first vs second chorus variation

Then:
- collect reasons, not just votes
- let artist respond
- test selected alternatives
- show the final choice

If a community idea is used, credit the contribution where appropriate.

---

## 5. Recurring characters

Build continuity through:
- recurring host
- returning artists
- Product Specialist / Engineering appearances
- community callbacks

A viewer should remember:

> “That was the feature Artist X asked for six months ago.”

This turns product development into serialized storytelling.

---

## 6. Creator roles

Do not force every collaborator into “brand ambassador.”

Possible roles:

### Creator
Brings artistic intent and new problems.

### Educator
Excellent at explaining EMU to other creators.

### Host
Can carry long-form narrative.

### Ambassador
Has reach and brand fit.

### Design partner
Gives unusually useful product feedback.

One person may hold several roles, but selection criteria should remain separate.

---

## 7. Success metrics

### Content
- view completion
- return viewers
- high-intent clicks
- qualified downloads

### Community
- creator applications
- high-quality problem submissions
- repeat participation

### Product
- validated problems from content
- experiments initiated
- shipped / killed decisions informed by the series

### Commercial
- licences / hardware influenced
- creator referral traffic
- earned media
