# Assumptions & Interview Validation Questions

## Why this file exists

This repository deliberately separates:

1. **published ENTTEC product facts**
2. **product hypotheses**
3. **things that must be validated in interview / internally**

A good product proposal should not confuse the three.

---

Public ENTTEC materials indicate:

### EMU
- targeted at musicians, DJs and producers
- audio-reactive workflow
- MIDI
- VST3 / AU / DAW integration
- Windows and Mac
- up to 32 universes on paid tiers
- perpetual licensing now available / earned depending on plan

- live media / generative effects

- professional / integrator / installation features
- Device Manager / Sentry in relevant editions

https://www.enttec.com/product/dmx-lighting-control-software/pixel-mapping-software/

## 2. Product hypotheses — NOT established company strategy

The following are proposals to validate:

- EMU should become the deliberate “Creator Lab” for experimental ideas.
- Mature EMU concepts should selectively graduate into ELM.
- ENTTEC should create a shared Creative Engine.
- ENTTEC should create EMU Artist Lab.
- AI should become a creative-intent translation layer.
- Product Specialist should own creator discovery and cross-product experimentation.

Do not present these as if ENTTEC already plans them.

## 3. Most important interview questions

### Broader role
> When the role became broader than EMU, what product problems or opportunities made the team decide to broaden it?

### Ownership
> What would I personally own in this role rather than support?

### Decision participation
> Could you walk me through a recent product decision a Product Specialist influenced, and who made the final decision?

### Broader portfolio
> Is the broader scope mainly about supporting more products, or about taking broader product responsibility across software and hardware applications?

### Creator discovery
> Would this role have direct access to users such as musicians, designers or integrators for product discovery?

### Experimentation
> If we wanted to test a small creator workflow or prototype with users before committing Engineering resources, would this role be able to lead that experiment?

### EMU / ELM relationship
> I noticed ELM 2026 includes a large set of shaders ported from EMU. How does the team currently think about product innovation across EMU and ELM?

### AI
> Has the team discussed using AI above the existing audio-reactive layer — for example, helping creators translate artistic intent or song structure into an editable lighting starting point?

Do not assume “yes.”

### Resources
> Which Engineering, Product, Design or Marketing resources would support this role, and how are priorities decided when teams are busy?

### Growth
> What would you expect this person to own independently after the first year, and how has the scope grown for strong performers in similar roles?

### International scope
> How does the Guangzhou team work with the other ENTTEC offices, and have comparable product roles moved internationally within the group?

### Work mode
> What does a typical week look like in terms of office attendance, remote work, customer visits and travel?

## 4. Signals that would strengthen the opportunity

Strong signals:
- named product / domain ownership
- direct user access
- Product Specialist participates in product decisions
- ability to run small experiments
- concrete Engineering access
- clear expansion of scope after 12–24 months
- examples of internal mobility
- explicit distinction between ownership and support

Weak signals:
- “you’ll touch many products”
- “we’re very entrepreneurial”
- “everyone wears many hats”
- “there are lots of opportunities”
- “you’ll support global teams”

These are not necessarily bad, but require concrete follow-up.

---

## 5. Signals that should lower confidence

- all responsibilities are reactive support
- no one can explain decision rights
- no protected product / discovery time
- Product Specialist cannot access users except through Sales
- Engineering capacity has no allocation mechanism
- scope grows only by taking on more support work
- international mobility is only a generic aspiration
- product ideas are collected but there is no decision / validation process

---

## 6. Interview positioning

Do not pitch the whole repository immediately.

A better sequence:

1. ask how ENTTEC sees the broader role
2. listen for actual internal needs
3. share one observation
4. test one hypothesis
5. only expand if the interviewer engages

Example:

> I noticed ELM 2026 includes effects that originated in EMU. It made me wonder whether EMU could be a useful creator-facing place to test new interaction ideas with musicians, while only mature concepts move into professional-scale workflows. Is that similar to how the team thinks about the products, or is the internal strategy quite different?

The purpose is to **learn**, not to demonstrate that the candidate already knows the company’s strategy.
