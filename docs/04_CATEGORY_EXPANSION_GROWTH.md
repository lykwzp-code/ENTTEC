# 04 — Category Expansion & Growth: Create New Lighting Users

## Growth thesis

The largest opportunity may not be limited to winning share from existing professional lighting-control users.

It may be to create demand among people who currently do not think of themselves as “lighting users.”

> **Instead of only competing for the existing lighting market, expand the market by making expressive lighting usable by individual creators and small creative teams.**

This is a market hypothesis to validate.

---

## 1. New users, not only competitor users

Traditional professional buyers may already compare:

- lighting-control software
- pixel-mapping tools
- consoles
- professional ecosystems

Competition there is largely about winning existing demand.

The new creator segment is different.

A dance creator may begin with:

> “I want this video to look more expressive.”

not:

> “I need a lighting-control platform.”

If EMU solves that first need, ENTTEC can enter before the user has formed a professional lighting-tool preference.

---

## 2. Priority adjacent segments

### Musicians / DJs / independent performers
Need:
- stronger visual identity
- simple show creation
- low dependence on specialist lighting staff

### Dance creators / K-pop cover creators
Need:
- more distinctive performance videos
- lighting that supports choreography and musical structure
- better differentiation on social platforms

### Dance studios
Need:
- premium recording / showcase service
- repeatable visual setups
- higher-value studio proposition

### Small creative teams
Need:
- rapid setup
- repeatable visual quality
- low technical overhead
- ability to grow without hiring a dedicated lighting specialist immediately

### Performance-content creators
Need:
- turn music into a complete visual performance, not just a recorded activity

---

## 3. Content-led market entry

Before large product investment, test whether this audience cares.

Working format:

> **Reimagined in Light**

The idea:

1. select rights-cleared music with strong identity
2. reinterpret it through light
3. show the creative reasoning
4. add dancers / performers where useful
5. publish the full process on YouTube
6. publish the final performance on TikTok / Shorts / Reels
7. observe creator demand and product friction

Content becomes:

- acquisition
- research
- product dogfooding
- education
- brand building

---

## 4. Why older catalogue music can help

Older music can offer:

- nostalgia
- existing visual memory
- fandom
- choreography / performance associations
- less direct competition with a current-release campaign

Example concept:

JYJ’s Get Out can be used as a design reference because of its memorable red visual language, performance staging and choreography.

The goal is not to recreate the MV.

The question is:

> **How would the emotional and spatial identity of an older performance track be reinterpreted today through light?**

Rights feasibility must still be assessed case by case.

---

## 5. Dance as a beachhead

Dance is a useful early market because it already combines:

**music + body + camera + visual identity**

The job-to-be-done can become:

> **Help me make my performance feel like a visual interpretation of the music, not only a choreography recording.**

This can reveal product needs such as:

- formation-aware moments
- centre focus
- choreography hits
- dance-break treatment
- final-pose freeze
- first vs final chorus variation
- camera-friendly lighting

---

## 6. Guangzhou as a Creator-Market Pilot

Guangzhou is a particularly practical place to test this category-expansion hypothesis because ENTTEC already has a China team there, while the city also offers access to creator communities such as:

- dance studios
- dance crews
- choreographers
- livestream / group-livestream teams
- MCNs and content operators
- performance-content creators
- independent musicians and producers

The strategic advantage is not simply geographic convenience.

It is the possibility of creating a **tight local discovery loop**:

**local creator  
→ EMU trial  
→ observed workflow  
→ product insight  
→ prototype / template  
→ creator retest**

That is much faster than starting with a broad global research program.

### Suggested first local cohort

A small Guangzhou design-partner group could include:

- 1 dance studio
- 1 dance crew / choreographer
- 1 livestream / MCN team
- 1 independent musician / producer
- 1 small creator / video-production team

The objective is not statistical representativeness.

It is to expose very different creator workflows early and identify where the same problems repeat.

### Two pilot tracks

#### Pilot A — Creator / Dance

Question:

> **Can someone without professional lighting training create a visually expressive result quickly enough to want to use EMU again?**

Test:

- templates
- onboarding
- Quick → Creator progression
- Music-to-Light controls
- camera-friendly lighting
- editing / publishing workflow

#### Pilot B — Livestream / MCN

Question:

> **Can EMU help high-frequency content teams produce more distinctive and repeatable visual environments without adding excessive technical overhead?**

Test:

- fast theme changes
- repeatability
- operator learning curve
- multi-song / multi-session workflow
- long-session stability
- template reuse
- content-team satisfaction

### Why this matters to the Guangzhou office

If the hypothesis works, the Guangzhou team is not only a regional support location.

It can become a **creator-market learning node** for ENTTEC:

- close to high-frequency creators
- able to run small physical tests
- able to gather direct user feedback
- able to validate new creator workflows before broader rollout

A useful internal framing is:

> **Guangzhou as a Creator-Market Pilot City.**

This is a hypothesis to test, not a claim that ENTTEC already uses Guangzhou this way.

## 7. Growth funnel

### 1. Spectacle
Short-form finished content:

> “I want my video to look like this.”

### 2. Curiosity
Long-form breakdown:

> “How did they make this?”

### 3. Learning
Tutorial / template:

> “I could try this.”

### 4. Creation
EMU + starter setup:

> “I made my own version.”

### 5. Sharing
Creator publishes:

> “Look what I made.”

### 6. Repeat / upgrade
Creator returns, buys more hardware, or takes on larger work.

---

## 8. Growth flywheel

**lower entry barrier  
→ more non-professional creators enter  
→ more work gets published  
→ more people discover the category  
→ more templates / community knowledge  
→ more successful creators  
→ more physical setup upgrades  
→ more ENTTEC hardware adoption  
→ some users grow into professional work  
→ ELM / professional adoption  
→ stronger case studies  
→ more creators enter**

EMU becomes not only a product but an **ecosystem entry point**.

---

## 9. Brand implication

The brand promise can shift from:

> “We make tools for people who already understand lighting.”

toward:

> **“We help creators learn to express with light.”**

This does not replace the professional ENTTEC brand.

It creates a creator-facing front door into it.

---

## 10. What would validate the category-expansion hypothesis

Strong signals:

- users arrive from creator / dance content, not lighting search
- creators ask to reproduce the setup
- template / tutorial use converts into EMU activation
- studios see commercial value
- creators continue after a collaboration
- some users upgrade physical setups
- some later move into more advanced ENTTEC products

Weak signals:

- views come only from fandom
- creators enjoy the shoot but do not want the tool
- setup remains too technical
- template users never customize
- hardware support cost exceeds value
- no repeat behaviour appears

---

## 11. Metrics

Do not measure only software revenue or video views.

Track:

### Acquisition
- creator-sourced downloads
- tutorial-to-product conversion
- organic creator referrals

### Activation
- time to first expressive show
- template completion
- first customization

### Retention
- repeat project creation
- repeat publishing
- creator independence from expert support

### Expansion
- hardware attachment
- setup expansion
- advanced-mode adoption
- ELM / professional progression where relevant

### Brand
- creator-generated examples
- community participation
- inbound creator partnerships

---

## 12. Strategic sentence

> **EMU can expand ENTTEC’s market by creating new lighting users, not only by competing for existing ones.**

# 中文版

## 04 — Category Expansion & Growth：创造新的灯光用户

### Growth thesis

最大的机会可能不只是从现有专业 lighting-control 用户中抢份额。

也可能是让一批原本根本不会认为自己是“灯光用户”的人产生新的需求。

> **不只是竞争现有灯光市场，而是通过降低门槛，让个人创作者和小型创作团队也开始用光表达，从而扩大整个市场。**

这是一个需要验证的 market hypothesis。

---

## 1. 新用户，而不只是竞争对手的用户

传统专业客户本来就会比较：

- lighting-control software
- pixel-mapping tool
- console
- professional ecosystem

这里主要是在争夺已经存在的 demand。

但 creator segment 不一样。

一个舞蹈博主一开始想的可能是：

> “我想让视频更有表达力。”

而不是：

> “我需要一个 lighting-control platform。”

如果 EMU 能先解决前一个需求，ENTTEC 就能在用户形成专业工具偏好之前进入。

---

## 2. 优先测试的邻近用户

### 音乐人 / DJ / independent performer
需求：
- 更强视觉身份
- 简单 show creation
- 不必马上依赖专业 lighting staff

### Dance creator / K-pop cover creator
需求：
- 更有辨识度的 performance video
- 灯光支持 choreography 与音乐结构
- 在社交媒体上拉开差异

### Dance studio
需求：
- premium recording / showcase service
- 可重复视觉 setup
- 更高价值 studio proposition

### 小型创作团队
需求：
- 快速 setup
- 稳定视觉质量
- 低技术负担
- 不必一开始就雇专职 lighting specialist

### Performance-content creator
需求：
- 让音乐变成完整视觉表演，而不是只记录动作

---

## 3. Content-led market entry

在大投入前先验证这群人到底有没有需求。

内容形式：

> **Reimagined in Light**

流程：

1. 选 rights-cleared、视觉身份清晰的音乐
2. 用光重新解释
3. 展示 creative reasoning
4. 合适时加入 dancer / performer
5. YouTube 放完整过程
6. TikTok / Shorts / Reels 放最终成片
7. 观察 creator demand 与 product friction

内容同时承担：

- acquisition
- research
- product dogfooding
- education
- brand building

---

## 4. 为什么旧歌有价值

旧歌可能同时提供：

- nostalgia
- existing visual memory
- fandom
- choreography / performance association
- 不需要和当前新歌宣发抢同一波 attention

比如 JYJ 的 Get Out 可以作为 conceptual reference，因为它有明显的红色视觉语言、performance staging 和 choreography。

目标不是复刻 MV。

而是问：

> **如果今天用光重新解释一首旧 performance track 的情绪和空间身份，会是什么样？**

版权仍然必须逐案确认。

## 5. Dance 为什么适合做 beachhead

舞蹈天然结合：

**音乐 + 身体 + 镜头 + 视觉身份**

它的 job-to-be-done 可以是：

> **帮我让表演成为对音乐的视觉诠释，而不只是把 choreography 录下来。**

可能暴露的产品需求：

- formation-aware moment
- centre focus
- choreography hit
- dance-break treatment
- final-pose freeze
- first vs final chorus variation
- camera-friendly lighting

## 6. 广州作为 Creator-Market Pilot

广州特别适合作为这套 category-expansion hypothesis 的现实验证入口，因为 ENTTEC 中国团队本身就在广州，同时本地也容易接触到不同类型的 creator community，例如：

- 舞蹈室
- 舞团
- choreographer
- 团播 / 直播团队
- MCN 与内容运营团队
- performance-content creator
- 独立音乐人 / producer

这里的价值不只是“离得近”。

真正的优势是可以形成一个非常紧的本地 discovery loop：

**本地 creator  
→ EMU trial  
→ 观察真实 workflow  
→ product insight  
→ prototype / template  
→ creator 再测试**

这比一开始做一个很大的全球 research program 更快。

### 第一批本地 design partner 可以怎么组成

可以先找一个很小的广州 cohort：

- 1 个舞蹈室
- 1 个舞团 / choreographer
- 1 个团播 / MCN 团队
- 1 个独立音乐人 / producer
- 1 个小型 creator / 视频制作团队

目标不是统计意义上的代表性。

而是尽早接触不同 workflow，然后看哪些问题会跨用户重复出现。

### 两条 pilot 可以分开跑

#### Pilot A — Creator / Dance

核心问题：

测试：

- 快速换主题
- repeatability
- operator learning curve
- 多首歌 / 多场 session workflow
- 长时间稳定运行
- template reuse
- content-team satisfaction

- 离高频 creator 很近
- 可以快速做真实物理场景 test
- 可以拿到 direct user feedback
- 可以在扩大 rollout 前先验证 creator workflow

一个很适合内部讨论的说法是：

> **Guangzhou as a Creator-Market Pilot City。**

这只是一个需要验证的 hypothesis，不代表 ENTTEC 现在已经这样定义广州 office。

- 用户不是从 lighting search，而是从 creator / dance content 进入
