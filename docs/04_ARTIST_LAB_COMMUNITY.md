# 04 — EMU Artist Lab & Community Co-Creation

## Mission

**Build tools with creators, not merely for creators.**

Artist Lab is the deeper stage that follows successful content-led market entry.

A collaboration should create three outputs:

1. a real artistic result
2. a product learning
3. authentic content

---

## 1. Design partner first, endorsement second

Do not ask only:

> “What feature do you want?”

Ask:

- What should the audience feel here?
- What visual behaviour would ruin the song?
- Where should light deliberately not follow the beat?
- Should the second chorus feel different from the first?
- What part of the song carries the emotional weight?
- If you avoid lighting terminology, how would you describe what you want?

The Product Specialist’s job is to translate those answers into a testable product problem.

## 1A. Music-to-Light Visual Grammar research session

Artist Lab should not only ask creators for feature requests. It can also be used to research a more fundamental question:

> **How does a musician break down their own music, and what visual role should each musical element play?**

This is deliberately different from technical stem separation.

The Product Specialist should not begin by saying:

> “Here are the drums, bass, vocals and synths. What colour should each one be?”

Instead, begin from the artist’s own perception:

> “When you hear this song, what are the elements you are actually paying attention to, and what is each one doing to the listener?”

### Suggested workshop flow

#### Step 1 — Artist-led song decomposition
Use one real song.

Ask the artist to mark:
- intro / verse / pre-chorus / chorus / bridge / outro
- important entrances and exits
- repeated sections that should feel different
- musical elements they personally consider important

The categories should come from the creator where possible.

#### Step 2 — Define the role of each element
For each important element, ask:

- What is this doing emotionally?
- Is it creating weight, motion, tension, intimacy, release, instability, emptiness or something else?
- Is it foreground or background?
- Should the audience consciously notice it?

#### Step 3 — Define the visual relationship
Ask:

- Should light follow this element directly?
- Should it influence space rather than brightness?
- Should it appear only at transitions?
- Should it stay visually silent?
- Should it be synchronized, delayed, drifting or in counterpoint?

#### Step 4 — Translate into visual primitives
Together define possible changes in:

- intensity
- colour
- spatial width
- focus
- movement
- density
- transition
- repetition / variation
- stillness

#### Step 5 — Build and compare
Create multiple small versions where useful.

Ask the artist:
- Which version feels most like the song?
- Which one is technically impressive but artistically wrong?
- What is missing from EMU today?
- What would make this workflow easier next time?

#### Step 6 — Capture the grammar
Update the Artist Intent Card with:

- musical element
- artistic role
- desired visual behaviour
- EMU implementation
- limitation / workaround
- artist evaluation

Over time, the team can compare these cards across creators and distinguish:

- reusable visual primitives
- genre patterns
- creator-specific preferences
- genuine product opportunities

This research is valuable even if no new feature is built immediately.

---

## 2. Pilot structure

### Duration
3–6 months

### Creator mix
Prioritise:
- strong creative point of view
- willingness to expose process and failure
- ability to explain intent
- real interest in visual expression

Mix:
- emerging independent artists
- mid-scale artists
- dancers / choreographers where relevant
- occasional high-profile guest

Large artists should amplify the program, not be required for it to function.

---

## 3. Recurring host / Artist-in-Residence

A recurring creator can become:

- host
- learner
- educator
- community bridge

The audience can watch that person grow from:

> “I am not a lighting designer.”

to:

> “I can now help another creator build a visual performance.”

A sensible partnership path is:

- 3–6 month pilot
- then a 12-month agreement
- extend only after the person proves durable as host, educator and creative partner

---

## 4. Episode structure

### Act 1 — Intent
Artist explains the song and desired audience experience.

### Act 2 — Translation
Team translates intent into:
- spatial language
- colour
- movement
- density
- transition
- sync / drift / restraint

### Act 3 — Build
Create the best possible version with current EMU.

### Act 4 — Friction
Show:
- awkward workflows
- missing capability
- failed ideas
- compromises

### Act 5 — Final work
Produce a real light-performance / audiovisual piece.

### Act 6 — Product learning
Record:
- problem
- workaround
- evidence
- next experiment
- status

---

## 5. The return-episode mechanic

A limitation can become a long-term story.

Example:

**Episode 4**
- artist asks for something EMU cannot express
- team produces a reduced version

**Episode 10**
- another creator tests an early prototype

**Episode 15**
- capability enters beta

**Episode 18**
- original artist returns with a new song
- builds something closer to the original vision

Possible title:

> **We finally built what they asked for.**

This makes product development visible and human.

---

## 6. Artist Intent Card

Each collaboration should leave a lightweight research artifact:

- Artist / creator
- Song / project
- Desired audience feeling
- Key musical / dramatic moments
- Visual language
- Current EMU method
- Friction
- Missing capability
- Workaround
- Evidence from other creators
- Status: exploring / prototype / not planned / released

Important:

> **Backlog is not a roadmap promise.**

---

## 7. Community as a discovery loop

### YouTube
Show the full creative process:
- intent
- experiments
- failures
- product limitations
- final result

### TikTok / Reels / Shorts
Show the strongest finished visual performance.

### Comments
Treat comments as **hypotheses**, not votes.

Example:

> “Can EMU understand lyrics?”

The product question is not “this got 2,000 likes, should we build it?”

It is:

> “What underlying need is this pointing to — lyrical meaning, narrative structure, section awareness, or something else?”

### Public responses can be honest

- “We’ll test this.”
- “We want to understand the underlying need first.”
- “We’re putting this in the exploration backlog.”
- “We tested it and it didn’t improve the workflow.”
- “This became a prototype.”
- “This is now released.”

---

## 8. Why this is more than content marketing

The loop becomes:

**creator intent  
→ real workflow  
→ visible friction  
→ product hypothesis  
→ experiment  
→ creator validation  
→ product change  
→ new content  
→ new creators**

If it works, content, research, product and community reinforce each other rather than operating as separate functions.

# 中文版

## 04 — EMU Artist Lab 与社区共创

### Mission

**和创作者一起做工具，而不是只为创作者做工具。**

Artist Lab 是内容冷启动被验证以后更深入的一层。

每次合作应该同时产生三个输出：

1. 一个真正完成的艺术作品
2. 一条产品学习
3. 一份真实内容

---

## 1. Design partner first, endorsement second

不要只问：

> “你想要什么功能？”

应该问：

- 这一段你希望观众感受到什么？
- 什么视觉行为会毁掉这首歌？
- 哪些地方灯光应该故意不跟 beat？
- 第二次副歌应该和第一次有什么不同？
- 哪一段真正承载情绪？
- 如果不用灯光术语，你会怎样描述你想要的效果？

Product Specialist 的任务，就是把这些回答转化成可验证的产品问题。

## 1A. Music-to-Light Visual Grammar 共创研究

Artist Lab 不应该只用来收集 feature request，它还可以研究一个更基础的问题：

> **音乐人自己到底怎样拆解自己的音乐？每一个音乐元素在视觉上又应该承担什么角色？**

这和技术上的 stem separation 不一样。

Product Specialist 不应该一开始就说：

> “这里是 drums、bass、vocals、synths，你觉得每个应该是什么颜色？”

而应该先从音乐人自己的感知出发：

> “你自己听这首歌时，真正注意的是哪些元素？每一个元素到底在对听众做什么？”

### 建议的 workshop 流程

#### Step 1 — 让音乐人自己拆歌
直接使用一首真实作品。

让音乐人标记：
- intro / verse / pre-chorus / chorus / bridge / outro
- 重要元素的进入与退出
- 哪些重复段落视觉上应该不同
- ta 自己认为真正重要的音乐元素

分类尽量先来自 creator，而不是产品团队预设。

#### Step 2 — 定义每个元素的作用
对每一个重要元素继续问：

- 它在情绪上到底做什么？
- 它是在制造重量、运动、紧张、亲密、释放、不稳定、空白，还是别的东西？
- 它是 foreground 还是 background？
- 观众是否应该明确注意到它？

#### Step 3 — 定义它和光的关系
继续问：

- 灯光应该直接跟这个元素吗？
- 它更应该影响空间，而不是 brightness 吗？
- 它只应该在 transition 出现吗？
- 它视觉上应该保持沉默吗？
- 它应该 synchronized、delayed、drifting，还是 counterpoint？

#### Step 4 — 翻译成 visual primitives
一起定义可能发生变化的维度：

- intensity
- colour
- spatial width
- focus
- movement
- density
- transition
- repetition / variation
- stillness

#### Step 5 — 做出来并比较
在有价值时做几个不同小版本。

问音乐人：
- 哪一个最像这首歌？
- 哪一个虽然技术上很炫，但艺术上完全不对？
- EMU 当前缺了什么？
- 下一次怎样才能更快做出你真正想要的东西？

#### Step 6 — 把 grammar 记录下来
把 Artist Intent Card 扩展成：

- musical element
- artistic role
- desired visual behaviour
- EMU implementation
- limitation / workaround
- artist evaluation

积累到多位 creator 后，就可以开始区分：

- reusable visual primitives
- genre patterns
- creator-specific preferences
- 真正值得产品化的机会

即使最后没有立刻做出新 feature，这个 research 本身也有价值。

## 2. Pilot 结构

### 周期
3–6 个月

### Creator mix
优先找：
- 有清晰创作观点的人
- 愿意公开真实过程和失败的人
- 能表达意图的人
- 真正关心视觉表达的人

可以混合：
- emerging independent artist
- mid-scale artist
- dancer / choreographer
- 偶尔一位高知名度嘉宾

大牌艺人应该是放大器，而不是项目运行的前提。

## 3. 固定 Host / Artist-in-Residence

一个长期出现的 creator 可以逐渐成为：

- host
- learner
- educator
- community bridge

观众可以看到 ta 从：

> “我不是 lighting designer。”

成长到：

> “我现在已经能带另一个 creator 做出自己的视觉表演。”

更合理的合作节奏：

- 3–6 个月 pilot
- 再签 12 个月正式合作
- 只有当 ta 被证明适合长期做 host、educator、creative partner 时才延长

## 4. 每期节目结构

### Act 1 — Intent

- colour

- transition

### Act 4 — Friction

做出一支真正完整的 light-performance / audiovisual piece。

### Act 6 — Product learning
记录：
- problem

- evidence
- next experiment
- status

## 5. Return Episode 机制

一个 limitation 可以变成长期故事线。

例如：

**Episode 4**
- 音乐人提出 EMU 当下做不到的表达
- 团队先做缩水版

**Episode 10**
- 另一位 creator 测试 prototype

**Episode 15**
- capability 进入 beta

**Episode 18**
- 原来的音乐人回来
- 换一首新歌，做出更接近原始想象的版本

标题甚至可以是：

> **We finally built what they asked for.**

这会把 product development 变成人真正能感受到的连续故事。

## 7. 社区作为 discovery loop

### YouTube
展示完整创作过程：
- intent
- experiment
- failure
- product limitation
- final result

### TikTok / Reels / Shorts
展示最强的最终视觉表演。

### 评论区
把评论当作 **hypothesis**，而不是 vote。

比如：

> “能不能让 EMU 理解歌词？”

产品问题不是：

> “2000 个赞，要不要做？”

而是：

> “背后真正的需求是歌词语义、叙事推进、歌曲结构，还是别的东西？”

### 可以公开、诚实地回应

- “我们会测试。”
- “我们想先理解背后的真实需求。”
- “我们把它放进 exploration backlog。”
- “我们试了，但发现没有改善 workflow。”
- “它已经变成 prototype。”
- “现在已经 released。”

## 8. 为什么这不只是内容营销

整个循环是：

**creator intent  
→ real workflow  
→ visible friction  
→ product hypothesis  
→ experiment  
→ creator validation  
→ product change  
→ new content  
→ new creators**

如果成立，content、research、product、community 就不再是几个割裂的部门动作，而会互相增强。
