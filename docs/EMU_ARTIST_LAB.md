# EMU Artist Lab

## Mission

**Build tools with artists, not merely for artists.**

EMU Artist Lab is a product-discovery program presented as a creator series.

Each episode should create three outputs:

1. a real artistic result
2. a product learning
3. authentic content

---

## 1. Pilot format

### Pilot duration
3–6 months.

### Suggested cadence
6–10 collaborations.

### Creator mix
Prioritise:
- strong creative point of view
- willingness to expose process and failure
- ability to explain intent
- genuine interest in visual expression
- no dependence on a full professional lighting staff

Mix:
- emerging independent musicians
- mid-scale artists with active communities
- occasional high-profile guest

Large artists should amplify the program, not be required for the program to work.

Aspirational examples may include distinctive artists such as LANY, Muni Long or Sia, but no artist should be assumed available until management, rights, budget and schedule are confirmed.

---

## 2. Host / Artist-in-Residence model

Start with one recurring creator who can become:

- host
- learner
- educator
- community bridge

The first episodes should show the host learning EMU rather than pretending to be an expert.

### Contract model
Recommended:
- 3–6 month pilot
- then 12-month agreement
- optional renewal / extension based on product and audience results

A 2–3 year commitment can be considered later if the person proves durable as host, educator and brand partner.

---

## 3. Episode structure

### Act 1 — Intent
Artist chooses one song and explains:
- what the song means to them
- what the audience should feel
- what should NOT happen visually
- the most important musical transitions

### Act 2 — Translation
Product Specialist helps convert intent into:
- spatial language
- colour language
- movement
- density
- transition
- synchronisation / drift / restraint

### Act 3 — Build
Create the best possible show using current EMU.

### Act 4 — Friction
Show honestly:
- what was difficult
- what EMU could not do
- awkward workarounds
- ideas that failed

### Act 5 — Final work
Produce a small audiovisual performance / light-show MV.

### Act 6 — Product learning
Document:
- validated need
- workaround
- proposed experiment
- decision: backlog / prototype / no action

---

## 4. Return-episode mechanic

One of the strongest storytelling formats is an unfinished feature.

Example:

**Episode 4**
- artist wants a behaviour EMU cannot currently express
- team produces a reduced version of the visual idea
- limitation is shown publicly

**Episode 10**
- another artist tests an early prototype

**Episode 15**
- capability enters beta

**Episode 18**
- original artist returns with a new song
- creates the version they originally imagined

Possible title:

> **We finally built what they asked for.**

This turns product development into a narrative arc and demonstrates that creator feedback can produce real product change.

---

## 5. Product-learning artifact per episode

Create a lightweight **Artist Intent Card**:

- Artist
- Song / project
- Desired audience feeling
- Key musical / dramatic moments
- Visual language
- Current EMU method
- Friction
- Missing capability
- Workaround
- Evidence from other creators
- Status: exploring / prototype / not planned / released

Important:

**Backlog status is not a roadmap promise.**

---

## 6. Example discovery prompts

Avoid:

> “What feature do you want?”

Use:
- What should the audience feel in this section?
- What visual behaviour would ruin the song?
- When should the lights NOT react?
- Should the second chorus behave differently from the first?
- What part of the song carries the emotional weight?
- If you could describe the light without technical words, what would you say?
- What did you have to learn that you wish the tool understood for you?

---

## 7. Program success criteria

### Product
- repeated creator problems discovered
- prototypes tested with multiple artists
- useful experiments shipped or deliberately killed
- reduction in time-to-first-expressive-show

### Creator
- artist says final output reflects intent
- artist can reproduce / modify the workflow
- artist uses EMU again without the ENTTEC team

### Community
- new creators apply to participate
- viewers create their own versions
- comments produce testable hypotheses

### Commercial
- qualified downloads
- licence / hardware conversion
- creator-led referrals
